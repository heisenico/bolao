## Design Context

Design decisions live in `PRODUCT.md` (strategic) and `DESIGN.md` (visual) at the project root. Read them before frontend work.

- **Register:** product (app UI; design serves the task).
- **Principles:** simplest-functional-possible; the app records/calculates but never moves money; fairness is provable, not promised; clarity over cleverness; mobile-first, match-day ready.
- **Anti-references:** gambling/betting sites; corporate SaaS dashboards. Copy is Brazilian Portuguese; target WCAG 2.2 AA.
